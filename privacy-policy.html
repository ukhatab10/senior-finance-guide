Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Senior Finance Guide is a free information website. This policy explains, in plain language, what information we collect when you visit, how it is used, and what choices you have.

Information We Collect

Information you send us. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write in your message. We use it only to reply to you. We do not sell it, share it, or add it to any mailing list. Please do not send us your Social Security number, Medicare number, bank details, or any other sensitive personal information by email.

Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting provider may record basic technical information when you visit, such as the pages you view, the time of your visit, your browser type, and a general location based on your internet address. We use this only to understand which articles readers find useful and to keep the site working. This information does not identify you by name.

We do not run accounts. This site has no sign-in, no user accounts, and no comment system. We do not ask you to register for anything.

Cookies and Advertising

This site is designed to show advertisements in the future through Google AdSense. At present, no advertising code is active on this site and no advertising cookies are set by us.

When advertising is enabled, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to you based on your visit to this site and other sites on the internet. You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. You can also opt out of a third-party vendor's use of cookies for personalized advertising by visiting optout.aboutads.info.

You can control cookies through your browser settings. Most browsers let you block or delete cookies; doing so will not stop you from reading this site.

Links to Other Websites

Our articles link to official government websites such as Medicare.gov, ssa.gov, and IRS.gov. Those sites have their own privacy policies, and we are not responsible for their practices. We encourage you to read the privacy policy of any site you visit.

Children's Privacy

This site is written for adults aged 60 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, please contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time, for example when advertising is enabled or when the law changes. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Continued use of the site after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Contact Us

If you have questions about this policy, you can reach us through the contact page.

Disclaimer: Senior Finance Guide provides general information only and is not financial, tax, legal, or medical advice. Program rules, income limits, and dollar amounts change from year to year and can vary by state. Before making a decision, verify the current figures with the official agency — Medicare.gov, ssa.gov, or IRS.gov — or speak with a qualified advisor or a free SHIP counselor in your state.
