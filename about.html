About Senior Finance Guide

Senior Finance Guide exists for one reason: the money rules that matter most after 60 — Medicare, Social Security, taxes, and retirement accounts — are written in language most people cannot follow. We rewrite them in plain English.

What We Cover

Our guides cover the topics that decide how much money stays in your pocket after 60:

  • Medicare costs and coverage — premiums, deductibles, drug coverage, and the programs that help pay them, including Medicare Savings Programs, Extra Help, and the Part B giveback benefit.
  • Social Security — when to claim, how the annual cost-of-living adjustment works, and what the earnings rules mean if you keep working.
  • Taxes in retirement — including the new $6,000 senior tax deduction for 2025 through 2028.
  • Retirement accounts — required minimum distributions, deadlines, and penalties.

How We Check Our Facts

Every dollar figure, income limit, and date on this site comes from an official source: the Social Security Administration (SSA), Medicare.gov, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), or the Internal Revenue Service (IRS). Every article lists its sources at the bottom, and every figure is dated, because these numbers change every year.

If a fact cannot be verified from an official source, we leave it out rather than guess. If two sources disagree, we say so, and we use the figure the official agency publishes.

What We Are Not

We are not affiliated with the Social Security Administration, Medicare, CMS, the IRS, or any government agency. We do not sell insurance, investments, annuities, or any financial product. No company pays us to recommend anything, and no article on this site is sponsored.

We are also not your financial advisor. Our articles are general education, written to help you understand the rules well enough to make your own decisions — or to know exactly what to ask a professional about.

Free Help Worth Knowing About

Before paying anyone for advice, know that free help exists:

  • SHIP (State Health Insurance Assistance Program) — free, unbiased Medicare counseling in every state. Find your local SHIP through Medicare.gov or by calling 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227).
  • Social Security Administration — call 1-800-772-1213 or visit ssa.gov for your own benefit estimates and answers about your record.
  • IRS Taxpayer Assistance — free tax help for qualifying taxpayers, including the Volunteer Income Tax Assistance (VITA) program and Tax Counseling for the Elderly (TCE).

Corrections

Program rules change, and mistakes happen. If you spot an error or an out-of-date figure, please tell us through the contact page. We check every report against the official source and correct confirmed errors promptly.
