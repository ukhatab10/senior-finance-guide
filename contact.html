Contact Us

Have a question about an article, spotted an error, or want us to cover a topic? We would like to hear from you.

Reach Us on GitHub

Send us a message through our GitHub repository — open an issue there and we will reply. We read every message and aim to reply within a few business days.

Please do not send personal information

Never email us your Social Security number, Medicare number, bank account details, or any other personal financial information. We do not need it to answer a question, and email is not a safe way to send it. If your question involves your own benefits or coverage, contact the official agency directly — the numbers below are the right place to start.

For Questions About Your Own Benefits

We cannot look up your benefits, check your eligibility, or give you personal financial advice. For questions about your own situation, contact the official agency:

  • Medicare questions: 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) or Medicare.gov
  • Social Security questions: 1-800-772-1213 or ssa.gov
  • Tax questions: 1-800-829-1040 or IRS.gov
  • Free Medicare counseling: your state's SHIP program — find it through Medicare.gov or by calling 1-800-MEDICARE.

Corrections

If you believe an article contains an error or an out-of-date figure, please tell us which article and which number, and if you can, the official source you are comparing it with. We check every report against the official source and correct confirmed errors promptly.
