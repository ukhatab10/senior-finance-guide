How We Write

Every figure on this site comes from an official source — the Social Security Administration (SSA), Medicare.gov, the Centers for Medicare & Medicaid Services (CMS), or the IRS — and every article names its sources. Figures are dated, because Medicare premiums, tax rules, and benefit amounts change every year. If a number cannot be verified from an official source, we leave it out rather than guess.

We do not sell insurance, investments, or financial products, and no company pays us to recommend anything. Our only goal is to explain the rules clearly enough that you can make your own decision — or know exactly what to ask a free counselor about.

Free help exists

Every state has a State Health Insurance Assistance Program (SHIP) offering free, unbiased Medicare counseling — no sales pitch, no cost. You can also call 1-800-MEDICARE (1-800-633-4227) or visit Medicare.gov for official answers.

Please note: Senior Finance Guide provides general information only. Nothing on this site is financial, tax, legal, or medical advice. Program rules, income limits, and dollar amounts change from year to year and can vary by state. Before making a decision, verify the current figures with the official agency — Medicare.gov, ssa.gov, or IRS.gov — or speak with a qualified advisor or a free SHIP counselor in your state.
